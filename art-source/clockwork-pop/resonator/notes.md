# cp_resonator-v1 notes

Built inline from the favorited concept `ComfyUI_00349_.png` (batch 469c3d53, "Shock Resonator Level 5"), reusing the Resonance Bell atlas and helpers from `cp_shock`.

- Iterations used: 2 of 8, contract passes on both.
- Static: plinth, three cream feet, coral flared skirt with two brass-ringed teal jewel ports, teal bell body and dome. Aim carries the brass stem and collar, amber crystal, floating halo ring with three studs, and the lavender and mint echo orbs. Muzzle on the axis at world z 1.20 (muzzle_forward off, like Shock).
- Atlas slots repurposed: champagne brass became lavender jewel resin, dark cavity became warm ivory, light facets became mint jewel resin.
- Iter 2: thicker, wider halo ring; larger orbs, studs and port rings.
- Counts: 6,626 triangles (iter 1; iter 2 similar), 13 mesh objects, 8 materials, 4 textures at 1024.
- Remaining flaws: orbs are opaque jewel resin, not translucent glass; bell is shorter than the concept; crystal reads pale in the checker; no vertex-colour AO.
- Self-score: 7/10.
