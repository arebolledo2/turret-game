# cp_quake-v1 notes

Iterations used: 6 of 8 (final = iter06, contract passed).

Final: 17,402 triangles, 9 mesh objects, 10 materials, size 1.81 x 1.79 x 1.66 m, min z -0.003.

## What failed / how fixed
- iter01-02: authored the layout in the wrong handedness for the harness camera: the hammer showed on the right of the
  three_quarter view and the lens-port face on the left (mirror of the concept). Also the stone ring was too big
  relative to the cube and the texture blotches were blocky.
- iter03: rebuilt the Aim parts in a mirrored authoring frame (reflect x,y -> -y,-x, normals flipped) so the three_quarter
  view reads like the concept: hammer + brass bracket + cream block on the left face, violet lens port on the right face,
  coral disc port behind-right, brass boom at the back-right top corner. Muzzle sits at the red-lens end of the head,
  y = +0.30 (passes muzzle_in_front_and_raised).
- iter04: rescaled to concept proportions (cube 0.70 m, ring about 1.7 m across, taller legs, hammer large).
- iter05-06: colour/gloss tuning, bigger cream block and head, chamfer restored after over-rounding the cube.

## Construction
- Static base: Base_Stone (7 wedge paving tiles + 10 chunky slabs + 3 loose chunks, generated cracked-stone texture),
  Base_Hub (indigo hub, swivel ring, brass plates, port), Base_Legs (3 coral cones, brass collars and domes).
- Aim (identity rotation, pivot on the vertical axis at z=0.52): Aim_Body (teal chamfered cube), Aim_Trim, Aim_Lenses,
  Hammer_Metal, Hammer_Cream, Hammer_Lens. Muzzle is a bare empty under Aim.
- Everything is generated by the script (stone texture is written to stone_gen.png by the script itself).

## Triangle / object overrun
Triangle count is about 3.5x the 5,000 advisory target: rounded bevels on cylinders and slabs, ~100 domed rivets, 15+
brass torus rings, glass orbs. Detail was kept for visual fidelity; first optimisation targets are rivet count, torus
segments and slab bevels. Object count (9) and materials (10) are within target.

## Remaining flaws vs concept
- Cube chamfers are smaller and less glossy-faceted than the concept; no baked AO or soft shadow, so the model looks flatter.
- Violet orbs lack the pink glow highlight; glass is plain translucent violet over a core.
- Stone slabs are plainer, cracks are texture only (no geometric chips), colour is lighter and less blue than concept.
- Handle is thinner and straighter than the concept, and the head reads as hanging a little off the body in side views.
- Hammer sits on the +Y face so the head axis is along X; the muzzle is at the -X (red lens) end.

## Self-score
7 / 10.
