# Pendulum Regent — final candidate

Five harness invocations, final iteration 5. Iterations 1 and 2 failed before export due to two small animation-script errors (empty Python range and an incorrect local variable); both were corrected. Iteration 3 passed all contract checks. Inspection identified rose backing occluding the dark cavity liner and the pendulum overlapping its lower lip. Iteration 4 moved the dark liner ahead of the cavity rear surface, raised the pendulum, enlarged the eye, and reduced unnecessary small-part tessellation. Iteration 5 strengthens the mallet attack and pendulum movement; it passes every contract check.

Final: **37,676 triangles; 6 rigid mesh objects; 7 materials; 2.220 × 1.072 × 2.220 metres; lowest z = 0.001 m.** Required empty pivots Body, Arm_L, Arm_R, Leg_L, Leg_R and Pendulum carry their own merged geometry. Front is Blender +Y. The triangle total exceeds the advisory 18,000 target because the rounded enamel shell, crown, convex shield, pendulum, toy feet and beveled brass fittings retain smooth close-view silhouettes. Small fittings were reduced from the original 59,172-triangle version without changing those silhouettes. Object and material counts are comfortably below targets.

Four procedural 1024² images: royal purple enamel, rose porcelain enamel, brushed polished brass and shared subtle roughness. Generated by this script, saved alongside it, packed in the Blender source, and embedded in the 4.30 MB GLB. No external image references. Every mesh has UVs at consistent box-projected density; the finish is clean and gently varied rather than weathered.

Rigid transform clips: **Idle 2.0 s**, **Walk 1.333 s**, **Swim 2.0 s**, **Attack 1.25 s**. Idle, Walk and Swim have matching endpoints. Attack anticipates, swings the mallet downward through approximately 112°, briefly crouches the body, then returns to neutral. The brass stem, hanger and bob rotate together around their top suspension pivot in every clip. `animation_audit.json` confirms imported GLB channels, durations, all four embedded images and endpoint errors below 6e-17. The parent agent owns Godot import/playback verification and animated preview; no game files were modified here.

Reference fidelity: purple-and-rose bell monarch, rounded three-point crown, one warm round eye, genuine recessed belly with a dark rear and side liner, visible top-attached brass pendulum, large domed violet/gold shield, clock-faced chunky cylindrical mallet, brass rivets and broad short toy boots. The invisible rear was invented with a small winding key/socket.

Remaining limitations: clean procedural surfaces lack the concept's complex studio reflections and painted flecks. The frontal shield is rounder than the concept's posed elliptical appearance. The mallet faces +Y to keep its clock face readable; the reference's barrel appears more diagonally oriented. Its attack is a visible animated downward blow, but gameplay contact/range timing has not been integrated. No baked AO, skeleton, gameplay controller or performance measurement. The hidden rear and underside are inferred from the single view.

Self-score: **8/10** for a recognisable polished candidate. Final visual inspection: `renders/iter05/compare.png`. Source: `build_cp_pendulum_regent.py`; packed source: `cp_pendulum_regent.blend`; export: `cp_pendulum_regent.glb`.
