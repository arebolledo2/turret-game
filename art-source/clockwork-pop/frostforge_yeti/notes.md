# Frostforge Yeti candidate v1

Final harness iteration: **4 of at most 8**. All contract checks pass. The final comparison image was opened and visually inspected, including the reference, front, three-quarter, side, back and top views. No game files or other asset runs were read or modified.

## Delivered model

- `build_cp_frostforge_yeti.py`: deterministic from-scratch Blender build.
- `cp_frostforge_yeti.blend`: packed source with rigid pivots and NLA clips.
- `cp_frostforge_yeti.glb`: self-contained exported candidate, Blender +Z up / +Y front; glTF Y-up conversion enabled.
- Height **2.163 m**, width **2.099 m**, depth **0.884 m**; lowest point **0.010 m**.
- **25,430 triangles**, **12 mesh objects**, **8 used materials**. The triangle count exceeds the advisory 18K target by 7,430 because the carved overlapping face/beard, domed shoulder shells, rounded armor transitions and mechanical hand joints need silhouette geometry. Initial 58,956 triangles were selectively reduced while preserving the face. This is a boss candidate, not a measured crowd-performance result.
- **4 embedded procedural 1024 × 1024 texture atlases**: albedo, metallic/roughness, normal and emission. All were generated by the build script. The atlas provides ivory ceramic, glacier enamel, dark hydraulics, titanium, brass, cyan reactor, deep teal housings and carved ivory families with subtle clean finish variation; no generic grime.
- Required empty pivots `Body`, `Arm_L`, `Arm_R`, `Leg_L`, `Leg_R`, plus child `Head`, elbow, hand and shin pivots. Each rigid assembly is consolidated; the palms contain three curled jointed fingers and opposed thumbs.

## Iterations and fixes

1. Full model and four clips exported; geometric/animation contract passed. Visual review found custom plates had a different UV-layer name from primitives, so joined plates sampled the ivory atlas corner. Blue shoulder shells were also buried within the larger ivory shell. The initial topology was unnecessarily heavy.
2. Unified every mesh UV layer to `UVMap`, exposed blue shoulder domes while retaining ivory skirts, tightened the neck collar, widened the torso/forearms, and reduced tessellation on small pieces. Contract passed; 48,986 triangles. Chest plates still appeared as isolated inflated lobes.
3. Replaced chest panel fans with curved ellipsoidal armor patches, broadened the torso/forearms, enlarged the jaw and layered beard, and simplified consolidated geometry with most detail retained on the face. Contract passed; 25,430 triangles.
4. Corrected animation direction so the two-arm attack lifts the fists in front and above the head, then leans into a forward slam; swim paddles now sweep forward. Final contract passes at unchanged geometric counts.

## Animation verification

Exported transform clips: **Idle** 2.0 s, **Walk** 1.2 s, **Swim** 2.0 s, **Attack** 1.4 s. Idle, Walk and Swim have matching endpoint transforms; Attack returns to rest. glTF does not store a universal loop flag, so the candidate playback wrapper should loop Idle/Walk/Swim and play Attack once. There is no root-motion translation.

`inspect_motion.py` re-imports the exported GLB and renders three key poses per clip; `motion_contact.png` and `motion_inspection.json` record those results. I opened the pose sheet and verified actual playback: quiet breathing, alternating leg/arm walk, paddle motion, raised two-fist wind-up, compressed slam. `asset_audit.json` independently records embedded images, clip durations, channel counts and endpoint differences. Godot validation is delegated to the parent task, as required by the brief; this run does not claim an in-engine check.

## Candid visual limitations

- The shoulders are more regular hemispherical caps than the concept's draped, tilted shells. Forearm armor is more rectangular and the body is more symmetrical.
- The central face is recognizably a stern mechanical ape/yeti with a short flat nose and sculpted ceramic beard; the beard blades are sharper and more regular than the concept's soft carved fur.
- The chest is broader after iteration 3 but the concept remains fuller through the waist and has more organic armor transitions. The character has humanoid biped/ape anatomy, three articulated fingers and an opposed thumb per hand, without a dog muzzle or quadruped anatomy.
- Small collar/shoulder trim and rear vent edges show some simplification. The invented back uses clean coolant vents and a ceramic spine; unseen construction is necessarily inferred.
- Texture variation is deliberately restrained and is difficult to see at gameplay distance. No AO bake, material animation, skeletal deformation, game integration or crowd benchmark is included.

Honest self-score: **7.7/10** against the supplied single-view concept. The major palette, shoulders, face/beard, power inset, mechanical hands and planted feet read clearly; the remaining difference is sculptural softness and exact armor flow.
