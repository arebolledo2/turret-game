# Clockwork Duelist Gunblade — frozen iteration 3

Original direct-3D design. The supplied saber image was used for clockwork material language and the articulated ivory mechanical hand, not its weapon shape. The result is an integrated gun-saber with a broad curved ivory blade, mint fuller, honed pale metal edge, substantial turquoise gun receiver above the blade, coral rotary ammo chamber, violet optical port, and an open short brass barrel. A bent forearm and ivory fingers grip only the dark rear handle. No strut or support touches the blade.

Three harness iterations used; all five standard views and each comparison sheet were inspected. Iteration 1 had incorrectly inflated validation/render bounds because the merged rigid material islands retained rotated object transforms. Iteration 2 baked these transforms, shortened the weapon 4% along Y, and enriched the teal/coral palette; all contract checks passed. Iteration 3 kept that silhouette and reduced insignificant bevel tessellation and optic tessellation: 18,132 to 13,956 triangles, preserving the large rounded forms and gun opening. Every contract check still passes. The final GLB and script are frozen and must not be rebuilt or edited after hash review without a new review.

Final: **13,956 triangles, 15 mesh objects, 9 materials**, no embedded textures, no exported animation. Dimensions **0.800 × 2.313 × 1.482 m** (Blender X/Y/Z). Lowest point **0.000 m**. Triangle count exceeds the advisory 5,000 target to preserve curved ceramic/enamel surfaces, articulated finger shapes, turned barrel rims and dimensional machining. Mesh and material targets are met; details moving together are merged by material.

Contract: `Aim` at Blender world `(0, 0, 0.565)`, identity rotation, centered on the static base. Bare `Muzzle` and `BladeTip` empties are direct descendants. Muzzle world `(0, 1.02624, 1.30)` sits just outside the actual dark-lined gun exit. BladeTip world `(0, 1.6128, 1.33)` marks the saber point, approximately 0.59 m beyond the gun exit. Everything above the axle moves with Aim. Static pedestal remains at the world origin and on the ground. The run-local `contract.json` adds `origin_node: "Aim"` to validate this intentionally asymmetric weapon without false bounds-centering requirements. Marker rotation is identity in Blender, forward +Y; GLB uses standard Y-up conversion.

Remaining limitations: no micro-texture or wear; this intentionally clean collection style relies on geometry and material contrast. The narrow edge thickness seen straight from the front/top is physically deliberate, while the side/three-quarter views carry the recognizable broad curved silhouette. Tiny screw slots and finger gaps will disappear at gameplay distance. Runtime swing/shoot behavior and in-engine import/readability verification belong to the parent's integration pass; they have not been executed inside this restricted run.

Self-score: **8.7/10** for strong hybrid identity, rear-only hand grip, dimensional machining and cohesive clockwork palette. The clean geometry is ready for runtime review; performance has not been benchmarked.
