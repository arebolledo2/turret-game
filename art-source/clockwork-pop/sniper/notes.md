# cp_sniper-v1 notes

Iterations used: 3 of 8 (final = iter03, contract passed).

## What happened
- iter01: built the full model. Contract failed only `origin_xy_centred`: the rifle sticks out 0.97 m forward but only
  0.585 m back, so the bounding box centre sat at y = +0.19 (limit 0.15).
- iter02: slid the whole rifle 0.11 m back relative to the swivel (pedestal pre-offset so it stays over the pivot; Muzzle
  adjusted). Also thickened the tripod legs and brackets, narrowed the spread, brightened the brass, enlarged the scope
  objective and barrel. Contract passed.
- iter03: made the coral forearm taller and wider so its side reads under the barrel as in the concept, slimmed the stock,
  enlarged the trigger guard, closed a gap between the scope eyepiece and tube, moved buttons/screws flush to the new
  widths. Contract passed.

## Construction
- Everything is generated by bmesh in one script (lathes for rings, bells, buttons, legs; bevelled boxes; (y,z) side profile
  extrusions for the coral forearm/receiver and stock). Parts are merged per (group, material) into 13 mesh objects.
- Base (static): `Base_*` meshes under root `CP_Sniper`. Aim empty at z = 0.70 (collar top), identity rotation, carries
  `Aim_*` meshes (pedestal, coral body, barrel, scope, buttons, trigger guard) and `Muzzle` (bare empty at barrel tip).
- Soft per-vertex AO is baked (subtle, floor 0.55) and exported as COLOR_0; lens materials are translucent.

## Remaining flaws vs concept
- Stock is a simpler hooked block than the concept (no overhanging nose/notch at the top front).
- Concept has painted-style highlights/shadows and lens glints; mine relies on plain PBR, lenses are flat blue/red.
- Details are mirrored on both sides (buttons, pill inlay, red scope lens) as the hidden side is invented.
- Tripod brackets are plain angular blocks, less sculpted than the concept's.
- No texture detail (brushed metal, panel lines).

## Counts and why they exceed advisory targets
- Triangles 12128 (target 5000): smooth 24-32 segment lathes for brass rings/bells/barrel and bevelled edges keep the
  curved brass and teal forms round; not optimized yet by design.
- Mesh objects 13 (target 16): within budget.
- Materials 11 (target 10): extra maroon ring material on the tripod column lock, plus separate blue and red glass.
- Size 0.867 x 1.535 x 1.278 m, lowest point z = 0.012.

Self-score: 7/10.
