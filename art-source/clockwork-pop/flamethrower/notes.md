# Clockwork flamethrower candidate

Frozen at iteration 6. Six harness calls total: iterations 1 and 2 failed during building because Blender 5.2's Boolean modifier introduced an empty material slot; iterations 3–6 exported, rendered and passed the contract. The builder now normalizes empty slots before joining. The checked contract is the local `contract.json`, copied from the provided soft contract with `origin_node: "Aim"` added to validate the real central yaw pivot of this asymmetric tool.

The first rendered candidate had a capped teal burner stub obscuring the bore. The final burner and brass heat guard are both open annuli, with a dark combustion chamber set behind them. Later passes strengthened the enamel palette, enlarged the sapphire/ruby controls, and reduced cylinder, small-vent and sphere tessellation without changing major silhouettes. Every render contact sheet from iterations 3–6 was inspected; iteration 6 is the accepted candidate.

Final counts: **12,408 triangles, 15 mesh objects, 11 used materials**, no images, no exported animation clips. Dimensions: **1.128 × 1.534 × 1.594 m**, minimum z **0.002 m**. GLB: **403,260 bytes**. The triangle count exceeds the advisory 5,000 target to preserve the rounded enamel tank, smooth wide hollow mouth, curved fuel hose, bevels, and rounded feet. The extra material preserves the amber ceramic-inset switch separately from the pink ruby and blue pressure lens. These are advisory overruns; all required checks pass.

`Aim` is an identity-rotation empty at the central base axis. The tank, hose, coupling pieces, neck, body, nozzle, pressure dial and side controls all descend from Aim and rotate together. `Muzzle` is a bare child of Aim at Blender `(0, 0.847, 0.89)`, with identity rotation and +Y forward. The four feet, low platform and static riser remain outside Aim. No modeled flames, armature, cameras, lights, or external resources are exported. Runtime aiming and flame effects belong to game integration.

Remaining visual differences: the tank and feet are a little squarer than the concept; the guard vents are recessed dark dots rather than drilled-through holes; studio highlights make the gold rim paler and the teal more muted than the reference. The lens uses a glossy opaque blue surface rather than transmission. Hidden sides were invented from the single concept. The looped hose and asymmetric service panel make the side/back silhouette more recognisable. Honest visual self-score: **8/10**.

Source/export: `build_cp_flamethrower.py`, `cp_flamethrower.blend`, `cp_flamethrower.glb`. The frozen file hashes are in `freeze.json`; final comparison is `renders/iter06/compare.png`. Native Godot import, yaw/pivot playback, muzzle checks at game camera scale, and resource/tests are left to the parent integration task. No frame-time claim was made.
