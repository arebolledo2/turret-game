# Grand Marionettist — final candidate

Final export: `eco_marionettist.glb`; source: `build_eco_marionettist.py`; packed editable source: `eco_marionettist.blend`.

**Three of eight harness evaluations used. Final iteration 03 passes every contract check.** Final comparison reviewed directly in `renders/iter03/compare.png`.

The model keeps the concept's humanoid skeletal clockwork puppet anatomy: a bone-porcelain skull with actual recessed circular eye holes and turquoise lenses, scalloped jaw, burgundy barrel breastplate, brass chest gem mount, transverse cylindrical shoulder reels, brass joint caps, narrow segmented waist, long mechanical legs, sturdy forward-facing feet and articulated clamp hands. A brass signal wand and thin hanging hand cables preserve the reference's secondary silhouette. Unseen back surfaces have a sensible vent panel and clockwork hardware.

Iteration 1 passed, but the armor and hand silhouette was too light. Iteration 2 widened the skull and armor, enlarged the fingers and simplified hidden/secondary tessellation. Iteration 3 corrected the attack axis after rendering the actual re-imported GLB: it now reaches forward in Blender +Y. No harness evaluation was used solely for inspection.

## Counts and spatial contract

- 35,114 triangles; 14 joined rigid mesh objects; eight used materials; 335 deliberately disconnected decorative components within those meshes.
- Dimensions X/Y/Z: 1.319 / 0.500 / 1.734 metres; minimum Z: 0.001 m; bounds center XY: -0.001 / 0.003 m.
- Body is the root empty. Head, Arm_L/R and Leg_L/R are present with geometry. Extra rigid child pivots Elbow_L/R, Hand_L/R, Knee_L/R and Foot_L/R allow articulation.
- Triangle count exceeds the advisory 20K target to preserve recessed skull cavities, round optic/gem lenses, large shoulder reels, curved cable silhouette and smooth brass caps. Mesh object/material/texture targets are met. No performance measurement or claim was made.

## Textures and export

Four script-generated 1024x1024 base-color PNG textures: Lacquer_Burgundy, Bone_Porcelain, Machined_Brass and Joint_Graphite. All meshes have active UVs; meshes without primitive UVs receive smart projection before joining. The maps add restrained broad coating variation and subtle directional machining rather than generic grime. Materials add appropriate metallic and roughness differences. All four PNGs are embedded in the GLB; there are no external URIs. GLB size: 6,132,880 bytes. No skeleton or skins.

`glb_audit.json` is generated by `audit_glb.py`, which parses actual GLB binary accessors and animation channels. It confirms actual changing rotation channels, stationary roots, endpoint equality and self-contained image buffers.

## Rigid motion

| Clip | Duration | Actual changing rotation channels | Intended playback |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Idle | 3.2 s | 7 | Loop, small head/arm/hand motion |
| Walk | 1.2 s | 11 | Loop, alternating long-legged gait |
| Swim | 2.4 s | 11 | Loop, arms sweep and legs paddle |
| Attack | 1.4 s | 7 | One shot, forward reaching command gesture, returns to rest |

There are no Body animation channels and no translation channels in any clip. All four clips begin/end at identical rotations (quaternion error zero or negligible floating-point error). Idle, Walk and Swim are authored as cycles. glTF does not encode playback loop state; set the three looping clips to loop when importing into Godot, and leave Attack as a one-shot.

`render_motion.py` imports the actual GLB, selects its imported NLA clips and renders key poses plus 16-frame sequences per clip. `motion/clip_poses.png` and `motion/Idle.gif`, `Walk.gif`, `Swim.gif`, `Attack.gif` show playback on a contrasting dark studio background.

## Honest limits

Visual self-score: **8/10**. The face and clockwork anatomy read well. The concept has a more painterly, faceted finish and a slightly richer asymmetric lower breastplate. This candidate has cleaner, more symmetrical plated forms, a simpler rounded pelvis, and small clamp fingers that are still less sculptural than the concept. The thin cables move rigidly with the hand pivot; they are not a simulated rope. The procedural textures emphasize polished finishes and do not include an AO bake or normal map. Side and back details were invented from the single view.

Actual GLB playback was checked in Blender. Game files were never modified. Parent handles the isolated Godot import/playback preview; Godot verification is therefore pending at handoff. No other run folders were read, and all writes stayed in this run folder.
