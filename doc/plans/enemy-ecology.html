Make the current cast smaller

Apply a 0.90 linear multiplier to the five existing types: basic, fast, tank, charger and exploder. Preserve relative sizes; heavies still read as heavies, with elite scaling applied afterward.

Scale visuals, hurtboxes, separation and contact footprints together. This reduces ground footprint by about 19%. Initially leave HP, speed, damage and explosion radius unchanged.

Preserve real core defense

The existing cast remains the core-assault group. Hero hunters and bosses never inherit the core-seeker flag. Siege enemies add deliberate core pressure at range.

In Survival, assault and siege enemies target the hero. In Convoy, siege enemies reposition around the moving core; locked shots still land at their announced positions.

Main trade-off: calmer enemies mean less guaranteed scrap and turret XP if ignored. Make sleeping beetles easy to find and kill. Retain the existing kill-scrap â†’ pickup-or-turret-XP choice; do not auto-award unearned scrap when the wave ends.

03 / Boss encounters

Simple â†’ layered â†’ elaborate.

All three bosses pursue the hero. Their attacks, summons and hazards cannot damage the core, and they never switch to it when the hero runs away.

Survive the round. Earn the kill.

Bosses are deliberately tanky, optional kill challenges. On waves 6, 12 and 20, spawn the boss early with a visible health bar and a curated 25% escort budget. Keep escort arrivals spread across the round.

The round ends when the timer ends, whether or not the boss is dead. A surviving boss retreats/despawns with its shots, hazards and summons; it grants no kill reward. Killing it early earns the reward, but does not end the round early. Normal salvage and the Shop follow the usual end-of-wave flow. Surviving wave 20 is still a valid victory.

In Convoy, preserve the existing arrival or safety-timer finish: the boss cannot hold up completion. Hero or core destruction still ends the run. Resolve hits on the final combat tick before cleanup so a last-moment kill awards exactly once.

Tanky through HP, not immunity

Use substantial health pools, with ordinary damage, burns and item effects still working. Tune HP against a fixed benchmark build for each boss wave: sustained focused fire from a representative build should consume roughly 80â€“100% of the available combat time. Movement and split targeting make a kill difficult; a strong build earns it sooner.

Calibrate per wave and difficulty, not against the player's live DPS. No adaptive health that cancels upgrades, surprise full heals or mandatory invulnerability gates. Boss HP is separate from the escort budget.

A reward worth pursuing

Brass Ram: guaranteed Epic item.
Prism Conductor: Epic, with a proposed 25% chance of Legendary.
Grand Marionettist: guaranteed Legendary.

Use the existing end-of-wave Keep / Sell review, including the 25% sell value. Respect mode eligibility, owned stack limits and pending reward reservations. If the required rarity has no eligible item, award its normal sale-equivalent scrap instead of silently downgrading rarity. No reward for merely surviving the boss.

Boss: dedicated HUD bar

A wide bar beneath the existing top HUD, visible throughout the encounter even off-screen. Name, current / maximum HP, small phase markers and a reward badge. Add a direction cue when the boss leaves view. Coral fill, dark track and a restrained brass frame; no huge floating 3D plank.

Chest: compact anchored bar

Replace the current world-space box meshes with a screen-space bar anchored above the chest. Constant pixel size with zoom, thin amber fill and dark outline. Show while damaged, targeted or nearby; hide on destruction. Do not rotate it with the model or let it obscure the loot art.

Health fill updates immediately; an optional short damage trail catches up over about 250 ms. Reduced Motion removes the trail, not the information. Clamp anchored UI to the viewport and handle overlapping chests. Neither bar relies on colour alone.

05 / Shared rules

Separate target, movement and attack.

Fairness is visible

Enemy shots use warm coral/amber cores with dark edges, distinct from friendly teal effects. Marks communicate timing through shape and fill, not colour alone. Directional weapons have a readable muzzle.

During normal combat, allow at most two nearby precision warnings and one major area warning simultaneously. Bosses own their attack sequence. Pause freezes warnings; Reduced Motion preserves essential timing and direction.

Hostile projectiles need explicit team and target masks. They hit the intended hero/core class once, never friendly turrets or their own allies. Enemy attacks must not trigger player-item procs.

Identity beats difficulty flags

Apply forced core-seeker and elite selection only to eligible assault roles. Difficulty never converts a lazy or hero-only enemy into a core attacker. Keep caps and minimum warning times on Brutal.

Use seeded wander, bounded leashes and range-band hysteresis to prevent threshold jitter. Share body-aware range tests with current combat. Clamp retreat destinations to navigable arena space.

Extract small target, movement and attack controllers rather than extending one huge chase conditional. Keep the shared crowd grid, hit pipeline, status effects and visuals.

06 / Delivery & validation

Prove the behaviour before producing the whole roster.

  1. First playable slice: shrink existing enemies, add Drowse Beetle and target-policy routing, and rebalance waves 1â€“5. Validate waking and losing interest with a temporary shell model.
  2. Ranged foundations: hostile projectiles, collision masks, shared warning controls, Hopper, Kite and Lobber. Validate every game mode.
  3. Summons and first boss: bounded Tender/skitters, timer-limited boss encounters and Brass Ram. Build the shared boss/chest health-bar presentation and high-rarity reward review.
  4. Boss expansion: Prism Conductor, then Grand Marionettist, built on the tested warnings, projectiles and summon caps.
  5. Art and balance: approve Clockwork-style 2D concepts, then promote to 3D with animation sockets. Tune complete runs alongside isolated encounters.

Acceptance checks

Brutal waves 1â€“5 are visibly easier and contain mostly meandering beetles. Beetles, Hoppers, Kites, skitters and bosses never aggro or damage the core. Lobbers visibly hold range and attack the correct target.

Target policies hold while the hero carries a turret. Smaller hurtboxes match art and preserve body-edge targeting. Pause/restart clears all attack and boss state. A living boss never blocks timeout or victory. Kills award once; timeouts award nothing. Every wave respects its roster whitelist, including substitutions under live caps. Seeded runs reproduce composition. Boss HP remains visible and chest bars stay readable across zoom levels.

Playtest measures

Early deaths and core damage; time spent chasing unreachable enemies; warning overlap; ranged enemies left at wave end; scrap collected versus turret XP; boss duration and failed-dodge causes.

Measure boss HP remaining at timeout and kill rates for weak, representative and strong builds. Strong builds should regularly earn the reward; ordinary runs can survive without securing the kill. These are tuning goals, not measured results.

Code findings behind this proposal

Current runtime selects basic, fast, tank, charger and exploder. Ordinary core-mode enemies switch between hero and core with aggro hysteresis; forced seekers ignore the hero. Rounds currently end at the timer/Convoy destination and clear enemies. Preserve that lifecycle for bosses; add explicit once-only kill rewards and timeout cleanup. Chest health currently uses two world-space box meshes in TechDropActor; replace those with the anchored UI treatment above.

  • CombatActor: chase, contact and hits
